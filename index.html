AI for Science · Multi-agent systems · LLM RL

Hi, I’m Owen.

I am a joint Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) and Shanghai AI Laboratory, where I am currently a research intern. I am advised by Prof. Wanli Ouyang and jointly mentored by Dr. Shufei Zhang. My current work focuses on multi-agent systems and reinforcement learning for large language models, especially for scientific reasoning.

Before my Ph.D., I received a B.E. in Artificial Intelligence from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). During my undergraduate studies, I worked with Prof. Renzhi Lu on time-series forecasting and Bayesian methods, and with Prof. Shan Tan on medical imaging.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in AI for Science, agentic systems, or LLM post-training.
