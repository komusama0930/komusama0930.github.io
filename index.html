Hi, I’m Owen.你好，我是 Owen。

I am a joint Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) and Shanghai AI Laboratory, affiliated with USTC’s Department of Electronic Engineering and Information Science. I am currently a research intern at Shanghai AI Laboratory, advised by Prof. Wanli Ouyang and jointly mentored by Dr. Shufei Zhang.

My current work focuses on multi-agent systems and reinforcement learning for large language models, especially for scientific reasoning. Before my Ph.D., I received a B.E. in Artificial Intelligence from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), where I worked with Prof. Renzhi Lu on time-series forecasting and Bayesian methods and with Prof. Shan Tan on medical imaging.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in AI for Science, agentic systems, or LLM post-training.

我目前是中国科学技术大学（USTC）与上海人工智能实验室（Shanghai AI Laboratory）的联合培养博士生，挂靠中国科大电子工程与信息科学系，现于上海人工智能实验室实习。我的博士导师是欧阳万里教授，联培 mentor 是张舒飞博士。

我目前主要研究多智能体系统与面向大语言模型的强化学习，尤其关注其在科学推理中的应用。本科毕业于华中科技大学人工智能与自动化学院；期间在卢仁智老师指导下开展时序预测与贝叶斯方法研究，在谭山老师指导下学习医学图像方向。

如果你对 AI for Science、多智能体系统或 LLM 后训练感兴趣，欢迎与我交流。
