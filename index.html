About

I work where machine learning
meets the natural sciences.

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China, advised by Prof. Wanli Ouyang and jointly mentored by Dr. Shufei Zhang.

My goal is to develop AI systems that do more than predict — systems that can reason, generate, and guide experiments. I am currently work on multi-agent systems and reinforcement learning for large language models, with an eye toward scientific reasoning and discovery.

Before my doctoral studies, I earned my B.E. from the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, where I was advised by Prof. Renzhi Lu and Prof. Shan Tan.
